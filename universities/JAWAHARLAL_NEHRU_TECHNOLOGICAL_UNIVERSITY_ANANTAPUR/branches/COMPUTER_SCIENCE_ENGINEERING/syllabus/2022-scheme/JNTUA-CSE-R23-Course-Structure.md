# JNTUA B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering — Course Structure (R23 Regulation)

> **University:** Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Anantapur (JNTUA)
> **Regulation:** R23 (NEP-aligned, effective from 2023-24 batch)
> **Program:** B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
> **Scheme:** Outcome Based Education (OBE) and Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)
> **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)

## Examination Pattern (R23)

- **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE):** 30 marks (mid-term exams + assignments)
- **Semester End Examination (SEE):** 70 marks
- **Total:** 100 marks per subject
- **Min passing:** 40% combined (CIE + SEE)
- **Question paper:** 10 questions (2 from each unit), answer 5 full questions (one per unit)
- **Units:** 5 units per subject
