# University of Mumbai B.E. Computer Engineering — Course Structure (R-2019 'C' Scheme)

> **University:** University of Mumbai (MU)
> **Regulation:** R-2019 'C' Scheme (CBCS)
> **Program:** B.E. in Computer Engineering
> **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)

## Examination Pattern (R-2019)

- **Internal Assessment (IA):** 40 marks (projects, quizzes, mid-term)
- **Semester End Examination (ESE):** 60 marks
- **Total:** 100 marks per subject
- **Min passing:** 40% combined (IA + ESE)
- **Min ESE:** 40% (24/60)
- **Question paper:** 5 units, all units mandatory
- **Grading:** 7-point scale (O/A+/A/B+/B/C/F)
