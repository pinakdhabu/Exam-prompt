# AKTU B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering — Course Structure (2021 Pattern)

> **University:** Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow (AKTU)
> **Regulation:** 2021-22 Pattern (AICTE Curriculum)
> **Program:** B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
> **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)

## Examination Pattern (2021)

- **Internal Assessment:** 50 marks (assignments, quizzes, tests)
- **Semester End Examination (SEE):** 50 marks
- **Total:** 100 marks per subject
- **Min passing:** 40% combined
- **Units:** 5 per subject
- **Question paper structure:** 10 questions (2 per unit), answer 5
