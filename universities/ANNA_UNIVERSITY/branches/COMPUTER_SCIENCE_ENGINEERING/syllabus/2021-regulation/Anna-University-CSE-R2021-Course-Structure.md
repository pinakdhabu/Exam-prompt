# Anna University B.E. Computer Science and Engineering — Course Structure (R2021)

> **University:** Anna University, Chennai **Regulation:** R2021 (effective from 2021-22 batch)
> **Program:** B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering **Scheme:** Choice Based Credit System
> (CBCS) — Outcome Based Education **Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)

## Examination Pattern (R2021)

- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA):** 50 marks (quizzes, assignments, tests)
- **Semester End Examination (SEE):** 50 marks
- **Total:** 100 marks per subject
- **Min passing:** 50% combined
- **Question paper:** 5 units, Part A (10×2=20) + Part B (5×13=65) + Part C (1×15=15)
- **Grading:** UGC 10-point (O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0)
