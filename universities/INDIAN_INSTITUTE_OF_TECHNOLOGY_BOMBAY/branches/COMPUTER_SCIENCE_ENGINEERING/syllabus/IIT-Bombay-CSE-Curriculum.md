# IIT Bombay B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering — Curriculum Overview

> **Institute:** Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB) **Department:** Computer Science and
> Engineering (CSE) **Program:** B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering **Duration:** 4 Years (8
> Semesters) **Grading System:** 10-point SPI/CPI (AA/AB/BB/BC/CC/CD/DD/FR)

## Examination Pattern

- **Letter grades:** AA (10), AB (9), BB (8), BC (7), CC (6), CD (5), DD (4), FR (Fail)
- **Min passing:** DD (4 points)
- **Assessment:** Quizzes (30-40%), Midsem (20-30%), Endsem (30-50%)
- **Terms:** Autumn (Jul-Nov), Spring (Jan-Apr)
