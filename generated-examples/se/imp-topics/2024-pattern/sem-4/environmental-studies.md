## Section C — IMP Questions by Marks

### 2-Mark Questions
1. Define environment.
2. What is e-waste?
3. What is EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility)?
4. Name two water pollutants.
5. What is an ecosystem?
6. Define sustainability.
7. What are the hazardous components in e-waste?
8. What is the difference between biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste?

### 5-Mark Questions
1. Explain the man-environment relationship.
2. Compare forest and aquatic ecosystems.
3. Explain air pollution — sources, effects, and preventive measures.
4. Explain water pollution — sources, effects, and the Water Act.
5. What is e-waste? Explain its composition and hazardous properties.
6. Explain the effects of e-waste on human health.
7. Explain technologies for resource recovery from e-waste.
8. What is EPR? Explain its role in e-waste management.
9. Explain administrative and engineering controls for e-waste.
10. What is waste reduction at source? Give strategies.

### 10-Mark Questions (ESE — likely essay-type)
1. Explain the complete e-waste management lifecycle — from generation to disposal to recycling. Include health impacts and regulations.
2. Discuss all four types of pollution (air, water, noise, solid waste) with sources, effects, and control measures.
3. Explain Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) in detail. How does it involve producers, government, and public?
4. Discuss environmental protection laws and compliance mechanisms for e-waste in India.
5. Explain resource recovery technologies for e-waste. Describe mechanical processing steps.

## Section D — Preparation Strategy

### Unit Priority
1. **Units III + IV** (E-Waste Management + Control) — **~50% of syllabus, guaranteed major questions**
2. **Unit II** (Pollution) — Important, easy to write
3. **Unit I** (Environment & Ecosystems) — Foundation
4. **Units III+IV are ONE topic** — study them together

### Key Strategy
- **E-Waste is KING** — Units III and IV together form ~50% of the syllabus
- **EPR** is the single most important concept — guaranteed to appear
- **Pollution types** — prepare a comparison table (air/water/noise/solid waste)
- **Case studies** — know at least one ecosystem type well

## Section E — Emergency Plan (1 Night Before)

**Absolute Bare Minimum:**
1. E-Waste — definition, composition, hazardous properties
2. EPR — explain in detail (guaranteed 5-10M)
3. Air + Water pollution — sources, effects, Acts
4. Resource recovery — mechanical processing steps
5. Health effects of e-waste

**Quick Memory Tricks:**
- **Pollution types:** **"AWNS"** = Air, Water, Noise, Solid waste
- **E-Waste hazardous components:** **"LCCC"** = Lead, Cadmium, Chromium, Chlorinated compounds
- **EPR parties:** **"PPG"** = Producer, Public, Government
- **Ecosystems:** **"FGDA"** = Forest, Grassland, Desert, Aquatic
- **E-Waste management lifecycle:** **"G-D-R-D"** = Generation → Domestic disposal → Resource recovery → Disposal
