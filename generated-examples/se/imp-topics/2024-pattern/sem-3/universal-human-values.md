## Section C — IMP Questions by Marks

### 2-Mark Questions

1. What is Value Education?
2. Define self-exploration.
3. What is the difference between happiness and pleasure?
4. What is trust in the context of relationships?
5. What are the four orders of nature?
6. Define professional ethics.
7. What is physical facility?
8. What is right understanding?

### 5-Mark Questions

1. Explain the concept of Continuous Happiness and Prosperity as basic human aspirations.
2. Explain the three levels — Right Understanding, Relationship, Physical Facility.
3. Differentiate between the needs of Self and needs of Body.
4. Explain the role of Trust and Respect in family relationships.
5. Explain harmony in the self and harmony of self with body.
6. What is Professional Ethics? How does right understanding form its basis?
7. Explain the interconnectedness and mutual fulfilment in nature.
8. Describe the Vision for Universal Human Order.

### 10-Mark Questions (ESE — likely essay-type)

1. Explain the complete framework of Value Education — from self-exploration to universal human
   order.
2. Discuss harmony at all levels — self, family, society, nature — with suitable examples.
3. Explain how professional ethics are grounded in right understanding. Give examples from
   engineering profession.
4. Discuss the current scenario of happiness and prosperity. How can we transition towards a
   value-based life?

## Section D — Preparation Strategy

### Unit Priority

1. **Unit I** (Value Education Intro) — Foundation, defines all key terms
2. **Unit III** (Family & Society) — Most relatable, easy to write about
3. **Unit IV** (Nature & Ethics) — Important for ESE essay questions
4. **Unit II** (Human Being) — Abstract but important for conceptual clarity

### Key Strategy

- This is a **values/theory** subject — focus on **understanding concepts**, not memorizing
- ESE is only 35 marks — likely 3-4 essay-type questions
- **Write from the heart** — examiners look for genuine understanding
- Use **real-life examples** to support your answers

## Section E — Emergency Plan (1 Night Before)

**Absolute Bare Minimum:**

1. Three levels — Right Understanding, Relationship, Physical Facility
2. Self + Body co-existence
3. Trust and Respect in family
4. Professional Ethics — definition + connection to right understanding
5. Four orders of Nature

**Quick Memory Tricks:**

- **Three levels:** **"URP"** = Understanding, Relationship, Physical
- **Harmony levels:** **"FSSN"** = Family, Self, Society, Nature (inside out: Self → Family →
  Society → Nature)
- **Human aspirations:** **"HP"** = Happiness + Prosperity
- **Values in relationships:** **"TR"** = Trust + Respect
