# Blockchain Technology — Sample Papers

## Syllabus

### Unit I: Mathematical Foundation for Blockchain

Cryptography: Symmetric and Asymmetric Key Cryptography, Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC),
Cryptographic Hash Functions: SHA256, Digital Signature Algorithm (DSA), Merkle Trees

### Unit II: Blockchain Fundamentals

History, Centralized vs Decentralized Systems, Layers of Blockchain: Application, Execution,
Semantic, Propagation, Consensus, Why Blockchain is important, Limitations of Centralized Systems,
Blockchain Adoption

### Unit III: Blockchain Platforms and Consensus

Types of Blockchain: Public, Private, Consortium, Platforms: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Hyperledger, IoTA,
Corda, R3, Consensus: PoW, Byzantine General Problem, PoS, PoET, PoA, PoB

### Unit IV: Cryptocurrency — Bitcoin and Token

Introduction, Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency, Cryptocurrency Basics, Types of Cryptocurrency,
Cryptocurrency Usage, Cryptowallets: Metamask, Coinbase, Binance

### Unit V: Ethereum Platform using Solidity

What is Ethereum, Types of Ethereum Networks, EVM, Smart Contracts, Implementing and deploying smart
contracts using Solidity, Swarm, Whisper, Truffle

### Unit VI: Blockchain Case Studies

Prominent Blockchain Applications, Retail, Banking and Financial Services, Government Sector,
Healthcare, IOT, Energy and Utilities, Blockchain Integration with other Domains

## Exam Pattern

- **ESE**: Units 3-6, 70 marks, 2.5 hours
- **ISE**: Units 1-2, 30 marks, 1 hour

## Key Topics

- Types of Blockchain (Public/Private/Consortium)
- Consensus Algorithms (PoW, PoS, PBFT)
- Cryptocurrency wallets (Hot, Cold, Custodial)
- Ethereum and Smart Contracts
- Solidity programming basics
- Blockchain applications in various domains
- Corda, Hyperledger architecture
- Bitcoin mining and transactions
