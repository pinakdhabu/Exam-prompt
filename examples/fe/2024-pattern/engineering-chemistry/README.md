# engineering-chemistry (2024 Pattern) — Sample Papers

**Course Code:** 107009 | **Credits:** 03 | **Semester:** I/II | **FE (2024 Pattern)**

## Syllabus Overview

| Unit | Title                                     | Hours |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------- | ----- |
| I    | Water Chemistry                           | 08    |
| II   | Corrosion and its Control                 | 08    |
| III  | Polymers and Plastics                     | 08    |
| IV   | Energy Sources and Storage                | 08    |
| V    | Analytical Techniques and Green Chemistry | 08    |

## Key Topics

- Hardness of water, degree of hardness, softening methods (zeolite, ion-exchange, reverse osmosis)
- Boiler troubles: scale/sludge, priming, foaming, caustic embrittlement
- Types of corrosion, dry/electrochemical corrosion, factors affecting corrosion
- Corrosion control: cathodic protection, metallic coatings, organic coatings
- Polymers: classification, types of polymerization, plastics (thermoplastic, thermosetting)
- Conducting polymers, biodegradable polymers
- Batteries: primary, secondary, fuel cells — Li-ion, Na-ion
- UV-Visible spectroscopy, IR spectroscopy, chromatography

## Exam Pattern

- ESE: All 5 units, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs | CCE: 30 marks
- 5 OR pairs (10 questions total)
