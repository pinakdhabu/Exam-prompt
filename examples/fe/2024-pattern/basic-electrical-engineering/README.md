# basic-electrical-engineering (2024 Pattern) — Sample Papers

**Course Code:** 102003 | **Credits:** 03 | **Semester:** I/II | **FE (2024 Pattern)**

## Syllabus Overview

| Unit | Title                                     | Hours |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------- | ----- |
| I    | DC Circuits                               | 08    |
| II   | AC Circuits                               | 08    |
| III  | Transformers                              | 08    |
| IV   | Electrical Machines                       | 08    |
| V    | Electrical Installations and Safety       | 08    |

## Key Topics

- Kirchhoff's Laws, Mesh and Nodal analysis, Superposition theorem
- Thevenin's and Norton's theorems, Maximum power transfer theorem
- Sinusoidal steady-state analysis, Phasor representation
- Single-phase and three-phase AC circuits
- Transformer principle, EMF equation, losses and efficiency
- DC motor and induction motor fundamentals
- Electrical wiring, earthing, circuit breakers, safety measures
