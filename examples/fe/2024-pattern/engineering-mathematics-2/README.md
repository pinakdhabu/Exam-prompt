## Syllabus Overview

| Unit | Title                                      | Hours |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------ | ----- |
| I    | Differential Equations of First Order      | 08    |
| II   | Higher Order Linear Differential Equations | 08    |
| III  | Partial Differentiation and Applications   | 08    |
| IV   | Vector Calculus                            | 08    |
| V    | Multiple Integrals and Applications        | 08    |

## Key Topics

- First order differential equations: variable separable, exact, linear, Bernoulli's equation
- Applications: Newton's law of cooling, growth/decay, electrical circuits
- Higher order linear DE with constant coefficients
- Method of variation of parameters, Cauchy-Euler equation
- Partial derivatives, chain rule, Jacobians
- Maxima and minima of functions of two variables, Lagrange multipliers
- Vector differentiation: gradient, divergence, curl
- Vector integration: line, surface, volume integrals
- Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem, Gauss divergence theorem
- Double and triple integrals, change of order, applications (area, volume)
