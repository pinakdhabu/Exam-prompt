# engineering-graphics (2024 Pattern) — Sample Papers

**Course Code:** 102011 | **Credits:** 03 | **Semester:** I/II | **FE (2024 Pattern)**

## Syllabus Overview

| Unit | Title                                        | Hours |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| I    | Introduction to Engineering Drawing          | 08    |
| II   | Projections of Points, Lines and Planes      | 08    |
| III  | Projections of Solids                        | 08    |
| IV   | Section of Solids and Development of Surfaces| 08    |
| V    | Isometric Projections and Orthographic Views | 08    |

## Key Topics

- Drawing instruments, types of lines, dimensioning methods, scales
- Sheet layout, conventions, lettering
- Projection of points in all quadrants
- Projection of lines (true length, inclinations, traces)
- Projection of planes (perpendicular, oblique)
- Projection of solids (prism, pyramid, cylinder, cone, sphere)
- Section of solids: sectional views, true shape of section
- Development of lateral surfaces of solids
- Isometric projections and views
- Conversion of isometric to orthographic and vice versa

## Exam Pattern

- ESE: All 5 units, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs | CCE: 30 marks
- 5 OR pairs (10 questions total)
