# Engineering Graphics — Sample Paper 1

**University:** SPPU (Savitribai Phule Pune University) **Pattern:** 2024 Pattern (NEP) **Program:**
B.E./B.Tech (First Year) **Semester:** I/II **Total Marks:** 70 **Time:** 2½ Hours

---

## Instructions

1. Answer Q.1 or Q.2, Q.3 or Q.4, Q.5 or Q.6, Q.7 or Q.8, Q.9 or Q.10.
2. Neat diagrams must be drawn wherever necessary.
3. Figures to the right indicate full marks.
4. Assume suitable data, if necessary.
5. Use of drawing instruments is permitted.

---

**Q1) a)** Draw the projections of a point P which is 25 mm above HP and 30 mm in front of VP. [4]
CO: CO1 | L2

**Q1) b)** A line AB 70 mm long has its end A 10 mm above HP and 15 mm in front of VP. End B is 50
mm above HP and 40 mm in front of VP. Draw the projections of the line and find its true
inclinations with HP and VP. [10] CO: CO2 | L3

**OR**

**Q2) a)** State the difference between First Angle and Third Angle projection methods. [4] CO: CO1
| L2

**Q2) b)** A line CD 80 mm long is inclined at 30° to HP and 45° to VP. Its end C is 15 mm above HP
and 20 mm in front of VP. Draw its projections. [10] CO: CO2 | L3

---

**Q3)** Draw the projections of a regular pentagon of side 30 mm having its surface inclined at 45°
to HP and one of its sides parallel to VP. [14] CO: CO3 | L3

**OR**

**Q4)** Draw the projections of a regular hexagon of side 25 mm having its surface parallel to VP
and perpendicular to HP. One of its sides is inclined at 30° to HP. [14] CO: CO3 | L3

---

**Q5) a)** A square pyramid of base side 30 mm and axis 50 mm long rests on HP on its base with all
edges equally inclined to VP. Draw its projections. [7] CO: CO4 | L3

**Q5) b)** A cone of base diameter 50 mm and axis 60 mm is resting on HP on its base. Draw its
projections when its axis is inclined at 30° to HP. [7] CO: CO4 | L3

**OR**

**Q6) a)** A cylinder of base diameter 40 mm and axis 60 mm is resting on HP on its base. A section
plane perpendicular to VP and inclined at 45° to HP cuts the cylinder. Draw the sectional top view
and true shape of the section. [8] CO: CO5 | L4

**Q6) b)** Draw the development of the lateral surface of a square prism of base side 30 mm and axis
60 mm. [6] CO: CO5 | L4

---

**Q7)** A rectangular pyramid of base 40 mm × 25 mm and axis height 50 mm is resting on HP on its
base. It is cut by a plane perpendicular to VP and inclined at 45° to HP, passing through the axis
at 20 mm from the apex. Draw the development of the remaining lateral surface. [14] CO: CO5 | L4

**OR**

**Q8)** Draw the isometric view of a pentagonal prism of base side 25 mm and axis 60 mm. [14] CO:
CO6 | L3

---

**Q9) a)** Draw the orthographic views (Front, Top, and Side) from the given isometric view of a
mechanical part having overall dimensions 60 mm × 40 mm × 30 mm. [8] CO: CO6 | L2

**Q9) b)** Explain the concept of Isometric Scale. Draw an isometric scale. [6] CO: CO6 | L2

**OR**

**Q10) a)** Sketch freehand the front view, top view, and side view of a stepped block with overall
dimensions 50 mm × 30 mm × 40 mm. [10] CO: CO6 | L2

**Q10) b)** Define: Isometric axis, Isometric plane, and Isometric scale. [4] CO: CO6 | L1
