# Savitribai Phule Pune University
## Systems in Mechanical Engineering (102003)
### 2019 Pattern | Semester II

**Total Marks:** 70 | **Time:** 2½ Hours

**Instructions:** Q1/Q2, Q3/Q4, Q5/Q6, Q7/Q8.

## Unit III — Vehicles and their Specifications (18 Marks)

**Q1)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Define **vehicle specification**. Explain following engine specifications: i) Power of Engine, ii) Cylinder Capacity, iii) Type of Transmission. [7]

b) Classify **automobiles** based on various criteria with examples. [5]

c) Write a note on **Electric and Hybrid Vehicles**. Compare with conventional vehicles. [3]

**OR**

**Q2)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain comparison of specifications of any two vehicles (e.g., hatchback vs SUV). [6]

b) Explain engine components: cylinder block, piston, connecting rod, crankshaft. [5]

c) What is **cost analysis** of a vehicle? List the factors affecting vehicle cost. [4]

## Unit IV — Vehicle Systems (17 Marks)

**Q3)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **steering system** with its types and working principle. [6]

b) Explain **braking system** — disc brake vs drum brake comparison. [5]

c) In a gear train, the driving gear has 20 teeth and driven gear has 60 teeth. Find speed ratio and torque ratio. [4]

**OR**

**Q4)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **power transmission system** of an automobile with neat block diagram. [6]

b) Explain **suspension system** — types and functions. [5]

c) Explain working of **differential gearbox**. [4]

## Unit V — Introduction to Manufacturing (18 Marks)

**Q5)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **conventional manufacturing processes**: casting, forging, metal forming. [6]

b) Explain **additive manufacturing (3D printing)** — process, materials, and applications. [5]

c) Write a note on **CNC programming** — basic concepts and advantages. [4]

**OR**

**Q6)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain machining operations: **turning**, **milling**, and **drilling** with sketches. [6]

b) What is **micromachining**? Explain its need and applications. [5]

c) Compare **conventional machining** vs **additive manufacturing**. [4]

## Unit VI — Engineering Mechanisms (17 Marks)

**Q7)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain working of **refrigerator** with its main components (compressor, condenser, evaporator). [6]

b) Explain applications of **gears**, **belts**, and **chains** in mechanical systems with examples. [5]

c) A water pump delivers 1000 L/min of water to a height of 20 m. Calculate power required if pump efficiency is 75%. [4]

**OR**

**Q8)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain working of **air conditioner (split AC)** with its main components. [6]

b) Explain applications of **springs** and **levers** in domestic appliances with examples. [5]

c) An electric iron rated 1500 W is used for 3 hours/day. Calculate energy consumed in kWh per month (30 days). [4]
