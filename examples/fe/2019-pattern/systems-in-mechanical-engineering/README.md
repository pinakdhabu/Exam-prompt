# Systems in Mechanical Engineering — Sample Papers

## Syllabus Overview
Energy sources, thermodynamics, vehicle systems, manufacturing, mechanisms, domestic appliances.

## Units
1. Energy Sources and Conversion — Thermal, solar, wind, pumps, turbines (6 Hrs)
2. Thermal Engineering — Laws of thermodynamics, heat transfer, IC engines (6 Hrs)
3. Vehicles and Specifications — Auto classification, engine specs, EV/Hybrid (4 Hrs)
4. Vehicle Systems — Steering, suspension, braking, transmission, safety (8 Hrs)
5. Manufacturing — Casting, machining, 3D printing, CNC programming (6 Hrs)
6. Mechanisms in Appliances — Gears, belts, compressors, valves (6 Hrs)

## Exam Pattern
- ESE: Units 3–6, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs | ISE: Units 1–2, 30 marks, 1 hr
