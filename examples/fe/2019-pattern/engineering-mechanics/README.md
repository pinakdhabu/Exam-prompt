# Engineering Mechanics — Sample Papers

## Syllabus Overview

Force systems, equilibrium, friction, trusses, kinematics, kinetics of particles.

## Units

1. Force Systems and Resultants — Resolution, moment, Varignon, centroid, MI (8 Hrs)
2. Equilibrium — Free body diagram, support reactions, beams (8 Hrs)
3. Friction and Trusses — Coulomb friction, ladder friction, method of joints (8 Hrs)
4. Kinematics of Particle — Rectilinear/curvilinear motion, projectile (8 Hrs)
5. Kinetics of Particle — Newton's second law, work-energy, impulse-momentum (8 Hrs)
6. (Review/Applications)

## Exam Pattern

- ESE: Units 3–6, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs | ISE: Units 1–2, 30 marks, 1 hr
