# Savitribai Phule Pune University
## Engineering Mechanics (101011)
### 2019 Pattern | Semester I/II

**Total Marks:** 70 | **Time:** 2½ Hours

**Instructions:** Q1/Q2, Q3/Q4, Q5/Q6, Q7/Q8. Use of calculator allowed. Figures to right indicate full marks.

---

## Unit III — Friction and Trusses (18 Marks)

**Q1)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) State and explain **laws of Coulomb friction**. Define coefficient of friction, angle of repose, and angle of friction. [5]

b) A ladder of length 5 m weighing 250 N is placed against a smooth vertical wall and a rough horizontal floor (\(\mu = 0.3\)). A man weighing 750 N climbs the ladder. Find how high the man can climb before the ladder slips. [6]

c) Determine the forces in all members of a **pin-jointed truss** using **method of joints** for a simple Warren truss of 6 m span, 3 m height, with a 10 kN load at the apex. [4]

**OR**

**Q2)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **ladder friction** with a neat sketch. Derive the condition for a ladder to be in equilibrium. [5]

b) For a given beam loaded and supported as shown, determine support reactions. [7]

c) Using **method of sections**, determine forces in members of a truss. [3]

## Unit IV — Kinematics of Particle (17 Marks)

**Q3)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Derive the equations of motion for a particle under **uniform acceleration**. [5]

b) A stone is thrown vertically upward with velocity 30 m/s from the top of a tower 50 m high. Find: i) Maximum height reached above ground, ii) Time to reach ground, iii) Velocity with which it strikes ground. [6]

c) Explain **curvilinear motion** with rectangular components. [4]

**OR**

**Q4)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Derive the equation for **motion of projectile** — show that trajectory is parabolic. [6]

b) A projectile is fired at an angle of \(30^\circ\) with velocity 50 m/s. Find: i) Maximum height, ii) Time of flight, iii) Horizontal range. [5]

c) Explain **normal and tangential components** of acceleration in curvilinear motion. [4]

---

## Unit V — Kinetics of Particle (18 Marks)

**Q5)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) State **Newton's second law of motion**. Derive the equation of motion. [5]

b) A block of mass 10 kg is placed on an inclined plane of \(30^\circ\). Coefficient of friction is 0.2. Find acceleration of block when it is released and distance traveled in 4 seconds. [6]

c) State **work-energy principle**. Prove that work done by a force equals change in kinetic energy. [4]

**OR**

**Q6)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) State and explain **impulse-momentum principle** for a particle. Derive the relation. [5]

b) A 2 kg ball moving at 3 m/s strikes a 4 kg ball at rest. If collision is perfectly elastic, find velocities after impact. [6]

c) Define **coefficient of restitution**. What are its values for elastic and plastic impact? [4]

## Unit VI — (Review/Applications) (17 Marks)

**Q7)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **centroid** of composite figures. Find centroid of a T-section with flange 100×20 mm and web 80×20 mm. [6]

b) State **parallel axis theorem** and **perpendicular axis theorem** for moment of inertia. [5]

c) Find moment of inertia of a rectangular section 200 mm × 100 mm about its centroidal axes. [4]

**OR**

**Q8)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain **Varignon's theorem** of moments. Apply it to find the resultant of a parallel force system. [6]

b) Determine the **centroid** of an L-section (angle section) 100×60×10 mm. [5]

c) A circular shaft of diameter 50 mm is subjected to a torque of 2 kN·m. Find maximum shear stress developed. [4]
