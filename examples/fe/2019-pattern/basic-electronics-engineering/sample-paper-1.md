# Savitribai Phule Pune University
## Basic Electronics Engineering (104010)
### 2019 Pattern | Semester I/II

**Total Marks:** 70 | **Time:** 2½ Hours

**Instructions:** Q1 or Q2, Q3 or Q4, Q5 or Q6, Q7 or Q8. Diagrams must be drawn.

---

## Unit III — Number System and Logic Gates (18 Marks)

**Q1)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Convert the following: i) \((10100.1011)_2\) to decimal, ii) \((832.24)_{10}\) to hexadecimal, iii) \((556.27)_8\) to hexadecimal, iv) \((2C6.1B)_{16}\) to octal, v) \((D43A.03)_{16}\) to decimal. [6]

b) Define **Universal Logic Gates**. Why are they known as universal gates? [6]

c) Compare **Microprocessor** and **Microcontroller**. [3]

**OR**

**Q2)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) State and prove **De-Morgan's theorems**. [5]

b) Implement **Half Adder** and **Full Adder** using logic gates. Draw truth tables and logic diagrams. [6]

c) Explain **SR Flip Flop** with truth table and logic diagram. [4]

---

## Unit IV — Electronic Instrumentation (17 Marks)

**Q3)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Draw and explain block diagram of **Digital Storage Oscilloscope (DSO)**. [6]

b) Explain working of **function generator** with block diagram. [5]

c) What is a **regulated DC power supply**? Explain its block diagram. [4]

**OR**

**Q4)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Draw and explain block diagram of **Digital Multimeter (DMM)**. [6]

b) Explain the working principle of **Auto-transformer**. [5]

c) Compare **Analog** and **Digital** voltmeters. [4]

---

## Unit V — Sensors (18 Marks)

**Q5)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain working principle and application of **LVDT** (Linear Variable Differential Transformer). [6]

b) Classify sensors into **Active vs Passive** and **Analog vs Digital**. Give one example of each. [5]

c) Explain working of **Thermocouple** as a temperature sensor. [4]

**OR**

**Q6)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Explain working of **RTD** (Resistance Temperature Detector) with material specifications. [6]

b) What are **optical sensors**? Explain working of **LDR**. [5]

c) Explain working principle of **strain gauge**. [4]

---

## Unit VI — Communication Systems (17 Marks)

**Q7)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Draw and explain block diagram of a basic **communication system**. Explain each block. [6]

b) Explain **AM (Amplitude Modulation)** and **FM (Frequency Modulation)** transmitter block diagrams. Compare AM and FM. [5]

c) Explain **cellular concept** in mobile communication. [4]

**OR**

**Q8)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Draw and explain block diagram of **GSM system** architecture. [6]

b) Explain wired and wireless **communication media** with examples. [5]

c) Explain **electromagnetic spectrum** and frequency band allotment for different applications. [4]
