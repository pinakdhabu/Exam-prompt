# Engineering Chemistry — Sample Papers

## Syllabus Overview
Water technology, instrumental analysis, polymer science, fuels, spectroscopy, corrosion science.

## Units
1. Water Technology — Hardness, EDTA method, zeolite/DM process, RO (8 Hrs)
2. Instrumental Methods — Conductometry, pHmetry, ion-selective electrodes (8 Hrs)
3. Engineering Materials — Specialty polymers, nanomaterials, graphene, CNTs (8 Hrs)
4. Fuels — Calorific value, Bomb calorimeter, proximate/ultimate analysis (8 Hrs)
5. Spectroscopic Techniques — UV-Visible, IR spectroscopy, Beer-Lambert law (8 Hrs)
6. Corrosion Science — Dry/wet corrosion, galvanic series, prevention methods (8 Hrs)

## Exam Pattern
- ESE: Units 3–6, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs | ISE: Units 1–2, 30 marks, 1 hr
