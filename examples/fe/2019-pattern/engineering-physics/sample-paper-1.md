   i) Electron &emsp; ii) Proton &emsp; iii) Neutron &emsp; iv) All of the above

   i) Matter particles &emsp; ii) EM waves &emsp; iii) Mechanical waves &emsp; iv) None

   i) Sum &emsp; ii) Product &emsp; iii) Difference &emsp; iv) Ratio

d) At \(T < T_c\), resistance of superconductor is _______ and conductivity is _______. [1]
   i) Infinite, zero &emsp; ii) Zero, infinite &emsp; iii) Zero, zero &emsp; iv) Infinite, infinite

f) Band gap ordering: [1]
   i) Metal > Semiconductor > Insulator &emsp; ii) Metal < Semiconductor < Insulator
   iii) Metal < Semiconductor > Insulator &emsp; iv) Metal > Semiconductor < Insulator

   i) Metals only &emsp; ii) P-type only &emsp; iii) N-type only &emsp; iv) All of the above

   i) 1–100 nm &emsp; ii) 100–1000 nm &emsp; iii) 1–10 μm &emsp; iv) >10 μm

i) **NDT** methods are used to detect: [1]
   i) Surface defects only &emsp; ii) Internal defects &emsp; iii) Chemical composition &emsp; iv) None

   i) Orbital motion of electron &emsp; ii) Spin motion of electron
   iii) Spin of nucleus &emsp; iv) Combined effect of i and ii
