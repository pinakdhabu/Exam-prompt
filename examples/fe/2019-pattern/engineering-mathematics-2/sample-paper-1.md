&emsp; iv) 1

iii) \(\nabla^2 F\) &emsp; iv) 1

ii) 1 &emsp; iii) 3 &emsp; iv) \(r\)

## Unit III — Vector Calculus (18 Marks)

**Q2)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Find \(\nabla \phi\) and \(\nabla^2 \phi\) where \(\phi = x^2 yz + xy^2 z + xyz^2\). [5]

b) If \(\vec{F} = (2x^2 - 3z)\hat{i} - 2xy\hat{j} - 4x\hat{k}\), find \(\nabla \times \vec{F}\).
Also check if \(\vec{F}\) is irrotational. [5]

c) Verify **Green's theorem** for \(\oint_C (3x - y)dx + (2y - x)dy\) where \(C\) is the boundary of
the square with vertices \((0,0), (2,0), (2,2), (0,2)\). [5]

**OR**

**Q3)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) If \(\vec{F} = (x^2 - y^2)\hat{i} + 2xy\hat{j}\), find \(\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r}\) along the
parabola \(y^2 = x\) from \((0,0)\) to \((1,1)\). [5]

b) Verify **Stoke's theorem** for \(\vec{F} = y\hat{i} + z\hat{j} + x\hat{k}\) over the surface
\(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\), \(z \ge 0\). [5]

c) Show that \(\vec{F} = (y^2\cos x + z^3)\hat{i} + (2y\sin x - 4)\hat{j} + (3xz^2 + 2)\hat{k}\) is
irrotational. Find the scalar potential \(\phi\) such that \(\vec{F} = \nabla\phi\). [5]

## Unit VI — Multiple Integrals (17 Marks)

**Q8)** Attempt the following: [15]

a) Evaluate \(\iint_R xy\,dx\,dy\) where \(R\) is the region bounded by \(x = 0\), \(y = 0\), \(x +
y = 2\). [5]

b) Change the order of integration and evaluate \(\int*0^1 \int*{x^2}^{2-x} xy\,dy\,dx\). [5]

c) Evaluate \(\iiint_V (x^2 + y^2 + z^2)\,dx\,dy\,dz\) over the volume of the sphere \(x^2 + y^2 +
z^2 = a^2\). [5]

**OR**

**Q9)** Attempt the following: [15]

a(1+\cos\theta)\). [5]

c) Evaluate \(\iiint_V xyz\,dx\,dy\,dz\) over the positive octant of the ellipsoid
\(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} + \frac{z^2}{c^2} = 1\). [5]
