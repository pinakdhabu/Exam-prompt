# Engineering Mathematics-I — Sample Papers

## Syllabus Overview
Calculus, Fourier series, partial differentiation, and linear algebra for engineering analysis. Covers single-variable calculus through multivariable optimization and matrix theory.

## Units
1. **Differential Calculus** — Rolle's Theorem, MVT, Taylor/Maclaurin series, indeterminate forms (8 Hrs)
2. **Fourier Series** — Full/half range series, harmonic analysis, Parseval's identity (8 Hrs)
3. **Partial Differentiation** — Euler's theorem, composite functions, total derivative (8 Hrs)
4. **Applications of Partial Differentiation** — Jacobian, errors, maxima/minima, Lagrange multipliers (8 Hrs)
5. **Linear Algebra: Matrices** — Rank, system of equations, linear transformations (8 Hrs)
6. **Linear Algebra: Eigen Values** — Eigen values/vectors, Cayley-Hamilton, diagonalization, quadratic forms (8 Hrs)

## Exam Pattern
- **ESE (End-Semester):** Units 3–6, 70 marks, 2.5 hrs
- **ISE (In-Semester):** Units 1–2, 30 marks, 1 hr
- **Question Structure:** Q1 compulsory (MCQ, 10 marks) + 4 OR pairs (Q2/Q3, Q4/Q5, Q6/Q7, Q8/Q9) each 15 marks

## Key Topics by Unit
- **Unit 3:** Euler's theorem, partial derivatives of composite functions, total derivative
- **Unit 4:** Jacobians, error approximation, maxima & minima of two variables, Lagrange multipliers
- **Unit 5:** Rank of matrix, consistency of linear systems, linear dependence/independence
- **Unit 6:** Eigen values & vectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, diagonalization, quadratic form reduction
