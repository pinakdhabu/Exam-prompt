Q1) a) Distinguish between combinational and sequential switching circuits. Write examples of both. [6]
b) Convert the following flip-flops:
    i) SR flip-flop to T flip-flop
    ii) JK flip-flop to D flip-flop [6]
c) What is a MOD counter? Design a MOD-7 counter using IC 7490. [6]

OR

Q2) a) Draw and explain a 3-bit asynchronous up-counter using JK flip-flop. Also draw the necessary timing diagram. [6]
b) What is the excitation table of a flip-flop? Write the excitation tables for:
    i) SR flip-flop
    ii) JK flip-flop [6]
c) With neat diagrams, explain the working of the following types of shift registers:
    i) Serial-in, serial-out (SISO)
    ii) Parallel-in, serial-out (PISO) [6]
