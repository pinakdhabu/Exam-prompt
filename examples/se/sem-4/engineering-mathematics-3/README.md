# Engineering Mathematics III — Sample Papers

## Syllabus Overview

Engineering Mathematics III covers linear differential equations, Fourier and Z-transforms,
probability and statistics, and numerical methods for algebraic equations, interpolation,
integration, and ODEs.

## Units

1. Linear Differential Equations (LDE) — 8 hrs
2. Transforms (Fourier and Z-Transform) — 8 hrs
3. Statistics — 7 hrs
4. Probability and Probability Distributions — 7 hrs
5. Numerical Methods: Algebraic & Transcendental Equations — 8 hrs
6. Numerical Methods: Interpolation, Integration, ODEs — 8 hrs

## Exam Pattern

- ESE: Q1 compulsory (MCQ from U3), then OR pairs from U4-U6
- Marks: Q1=10, U4=18, U5=18, U6=17

## Key Topics

- Bayes' theorem, binomial/Poisson/normal distribution
- Newton-Raphson, Regula-Falsi, Gauss-Seidel methods
- Newton's forward/backward, Lagrange interpolation
- Simpson's rule, Runge-Kutta 4th order
- Correlation, regression, curve fitting
- Fourier and Z-transforms
