## Examiner Commentary
This paper mirrors the WT April 2024 PYQ format. Q1(a-b) covers servlets and XML fundamentals. Q3(a-b) tests JSP/MVC and Struts framework knowledge. Q5/Q6 focus on server-side scripting (PHP) and .NET/Node.js concepts. Q7/Q8 cover Ruby and Rails — the final unit. Each OR pair has a mix of practical code explanation and conceptual comparison questions.
