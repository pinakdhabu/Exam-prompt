### Unit IV — Predictive Big Data Analytics with Python

**Q3)** **a)** What is logistic regression? How does it differ from linear regression? Explain the
sigmoid function and its role in logistic regression. [9]

**b)** Suppose you are given a dataset of emails classified as spam or not spam with features
"offer" (1=present, 0=absent) and "free" (1=present, 0=absent). A new email has offer=1, free=1.

| Email | Offer | Free | Spam |
| ----- | ----- | ---- | ---- |
| 1     | 1     | 0    | No   |
| 2     | 0     | 1    | Yes  |
| 3     | 1     | 1    | Yes  |
| 4     | 0     | 1    | No   |
| 5     | 1     | 1    | Yes  |

Calculate the probability that the new email is spam using Naive Bayes. [9]

**OR**

**Q4)** **a)** How does the Apriori algorithm discover frequent itemsets? Explain the role of
support and confidence in association rule mining. [9]

**b)** Explain the process of building a decision tree. What are the criteria used for splitting
nodes (Gini index, entropy, information gain)? [9]
