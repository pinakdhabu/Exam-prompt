## Key Topics

- Minimax algorithm, alpha-beta pruning, game theory
- Constraint satisfaction problems (CSP), cryptarithmetic
- Agents: types, knowledge-based agents, Wumpus World
- Knowledge engineering, FOL, unification, forward/backward chaining
- Ontological engineering, STRIPS planning, goal stack planning
- Planning in deterministic and non-deterministic domains
- AI components, architectures, and real-world applications
