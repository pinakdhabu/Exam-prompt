## Examiner Commentary

This paper follows the standard SPPU CN pattern — short note-heavy with numerical subnetting and UDP
header parsing. Q1(c) tests practical IPv4 subnetting. Q3(c) requires parsing a real UDP dump. Each
OR pair covers one unit with balanced theory and numerical components. Security questions (Q7/Q8)
include both architectural (X.800) and applied (SSL, Firewalls) knowledge.
