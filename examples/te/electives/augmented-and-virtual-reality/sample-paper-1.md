### Unit III — Representing and Rendering the Virtual World

**Q1)** **a)** Explain the visual representation and rendering process in VR. Describe the rendering pipeline: modeling, transformation, clipping, projection, rasterization, and display. [9]

**b)** Compare geometric representation and modeling techniques used in VR. Explain polygon meshes, NURBS, and voxel representations with their advantages and disadvantages. [9]

**OR**

**Q2)** **a)** Explain aural (audio) representation and rendering in VR. Discuss 3D audio techniques: binaural audio, HRTF, and spatial audio. Why is audio important for immersion? [9]

**b)** What is haptic rendering? Explain the types of haptic feedback: tactile, kinesthetic, and force feedback. Discuss haptic devices used in VR systems. [9]

---

### Unit IV — Interaction and Experience in VR

**Q3)** **a)** Explain user interface metaphors used in VR. Describe manipulation techniques: direct user control, physical control, and virtual control with examples. [9]

**b)** Discuss navigation techniques in VR. Explain walking-based (real walking, redirected walking) and steering-based (gaze-directed, pointing, torso-directed) navigation. [8]

**OR**

**Q4)** **a)** What is immersion and presence in VR? Explain factors affecting presence: field of view, resolution, latency, update rate, and tracking fidelity. [9]

**b)** Explain VR sickness (cybersickness). Discuss its causes (vection, sensory conflict, latency) and mitigation techniques. [8]

---

### Unit V — Augmented Reality

**Q5)** **a)** Explain the fundamental concepts of Augmented Reality (AR). Describe the difference between AR, VR, and MR (Mixed Reality) with a comparison table. [9]

**b)** What are depth cues in AR? Explain monocular and binocular depth cues. Discuss the problem of registration and latency in AR systems. [9]

**OR**

**Q6)** **a)** Describe AR hardware components: sensors, processors, displays. Compare optical see-through and video see-through HMDs for AR applications. [9]

**b)** Explain AR tracking technologies: magnetic, optical (marker-based), GPS, and hybrid tracking. Discuss the advantages and limitations of each approach. [9]

---

### Unit VI — AR Software and Mobile AR

**Q7)** **a)** Explain the software components and tools for AR development. Describe ARKit (iOS), ARCore (Android), and Vuforia with their features and capabilities. [9]

**b)** Differentiate between marker-based and markerless AR tracking. Explain how SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) enables markerless AR. [8]

**OR**

**Q8)** **a)** Discuss mobile AR platforms and frameworks. Explain the process of creating a mobile AR application using ARKit/ARCore with a step-by-step approach. [9]

**b)** Write short notes on any two: i) Industrial applications of AR ii) Medical applications of VR iii) AR in education and training [8]

## Examiner Commentary

This paper covers VR rendering (visual, aural, haptic), VR interaction and experience (navigation, immersion, cybersickness), AR fundamentals (registration, depth cues, tracking), and AR development (tools, SLAM, mobile AR). The comparison questions (AR vs VR vs MR, marker-based vs markerless) and application-focused questions ensure comprehensive assessment.
